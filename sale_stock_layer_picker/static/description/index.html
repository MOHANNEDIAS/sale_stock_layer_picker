Advanced Cost Layer Selection for Odoo 18

Stop leaking profits. Don't let standard FIFO logic dictate your margins. Take full control over which inventory batch is sold and at what cost.

The Problem: FIFO Blind Spots

In volatile markets, purchase prices fluctuate constantly. Standard Odoo logic (FIFO) automatically picks the oldest cost.

The Risk: You might be selling a product believing you have a high margin, but Odoo accounts for an older, cheaper cost layer. When you restock at higher prices, your realized profit is lower than expected, leading to misleading financial reports.

The Solution: Precision Picking

Empower your sales team with Granular Inventory Visibility. This module allows you to see every available cost layer (batch) and select exactly which one to deplete.

The Result: The system forces the accounting entries to match your selection, ensuring your Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is 100% accurate to the specific batch you intended to sell.

Seamless Workflow

1. Open The Picker

On the Sales Order line, simply click the list icon to open the availability wizard and see all your stock layers.

2. Allocate Quantity

View entry dates and costs. Split your required quantity across the most strategic layers manually.

3. Auto-Validation

Upon delivery, the system overrides FIFO and strictly depletes the chosen layers. Accounting is handled automatically.
